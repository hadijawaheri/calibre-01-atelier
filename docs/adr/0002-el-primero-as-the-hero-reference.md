# 0002. Zenith El Primero 400 as the reference movement

Status: accepted

The opening animation had to be inspired by a real calibre. El Primero 400 (1969) was chosen because its numbers are
distinctive and well documented: 36,000 vph (5 Hz, ten beats a second), 31 jewels, 50+ hours of reserve, 278 parts,
a column-wheel chronograph. Those numbers drive the animation (balance tempo, tick sound, a chronograph that resolves
tenths of a second).

The drawing is an artistic interpretation. Kronos is a fictional brand and the site says it has no affiliation with
Zenith.
